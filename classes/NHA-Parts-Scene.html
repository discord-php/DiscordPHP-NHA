Scene extends Out
in package
  • Application

Scene.php : 43

A lightweight, read-only wrapper around a single `GET /scene` response (the `SceneOut` schema): the 3D World-tab scene graph.

The biomes/deposits static layers are sent only for ?static=1 (see WorldRepository::getScene()) and are null otherwise — do not treat a missing layer as "empty".
